Inspector 0.6.8 is made for iPhone Duo, and needs iOS 15 or later.

- Inspector is built against the iOS 27.1 SDK. On iPhone Duo the process list's menus, the live-updates switch and Search run down the side under the status bar, instead of a top bar crowding the status icons; on the inner display the process list and the process you picked sit side by side.
- The "N in total" line under a list and the credits under the process list keep to the safe area, so neither the side bar nor the sidebar covers them.
- The sheet for a row closes with the system's Done.
- iOS 13 and 14 are no longer supported: 0.6.7 is the last release for them. The tools that build for iOS 27.1 do not build for anything older than iOS 15.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for custom firmware with a roothide bootstrap or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless (`/var/jb`). SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
