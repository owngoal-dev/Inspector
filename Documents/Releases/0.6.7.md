Inspector 0.6.7 opens a screen with its records already in hand.

- Tapping a process, or a thread, file, port or module under “More Details”, now waits at the tap for the records and then opens a finished screen, instead of pushing an empty one that fills in a moment later. The row under the finger stays highlighted while it waits, and while the screen it opened is up.
- Launch holds the app’s mark and background on screen until the first sample lands, then dissolves into the process list, rather than opening on an empty list.
- The count badge beside “Mach Ports” is a capsule again; its corner had been taken from the height the badge had while it was still hidden.
- The “N in total” line under a list’s last row sits at the same margin as the rows again.
