Inspector 0.6.6 is a smaller download.

- The app's images took 4.5 MB of the package, nearly all of it six large copies of the app icon. The icon now ships as three compressed images, and the images take 0.4 MB. It looks the same on the Home Screen in Default, Dark, Clear and Tinted; it no longer moves with the light the way the layered glass icon did.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for custom firmware with a roothide bootstrap or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless (`/var/jb`). SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
